How OmniClaim works

  1. Upload and photo checksClaim form, damage photo and bill. Camera metadata and a perceptual-hash search of every earlier claim for reused photos.
  2. Bill readingThe Document AI Invoice Parser extracts every line item, total and invoice number. Without it, Gemini reads the bill.
  3. Policy and cost checksPolicy period and holder from the register; amount compared with similar past claims (BigQuery or a local copy).
  4. Gemini assessmentGemini judges each billed line against the photo and the policy wording and returns a structured assessment.
  5. RulesPlain code routes the claim and calculates the settlement, so the numbers always add up.
  6. AdjusterReviews the evidence, decides, and sends the letter. The copilot answers questions about the claim.
