The claims built around these photos are fictional. The people and businesses in the photos have nothing to do with them.

## Documents

`documents/*.png` are fictional invoices made for this demo with `tools/make_sample_documents.py`. All businesses, people, policy numbers and VINs in them are invented.

## Presets

`presets.json` defines the four demo scenarios in the "New claim" dialog. Each one lists the form values and which photo and document to attach.
